# Context

Glossary for ATC. Terms are used as defined here in code, tests and UI copy.

## Terms

- **Diorama**: the three.js 3D scene of one airport (ground, pavement, markings, extruded buildings, aircraft), drawn in a canvas of its own over the World map (not a MapLibre custom layer) and kept on the same camera, the two converted into each other's terms each frame; during a **Handover** it crossfades over the map. Code in `src/lib/scene/`.
- **World map**: the MapLibre view showing each airport with its live aircraft count; zooming in hands over to the diorama. Code in `src/lib/globe/`.
- **Handover**: the continuous zoom from the world map into an airport's diorama, with no page change. An airport **picked** (in the header's picker or the search) is flown to and landed on: while that flight is under way the Handover lands on no other airport, the one just left included (`switchesToLanding`, `src/lib/globe/handover.ts`; `GlobeMap.flyingTo`).
- **Journey**: following one flight gate to gate: out of its origin's diorama, across the map, into its destination's.
- **En-route view**: the 3D view of a followed flight on the World map, between its two Dioramas: the map tilted round the aircraft over 3D terrain, with the aircraft's class model drawn at its true position, altitude, heading and climb attitude in a MapLibre custom layer that shares the map's depth buffer (docs/adr/0002-en-route-custom-layer.md). Its **Camera** is picked on the Journey card: **Map** (the flat World map, where Following starts), **Chase** (behind and above the aircraft along its track, looking down at it), **Cockpit** (at the nose, looking ahead, pitched with the climb or descent), **Side** (abeam on the left at wing height) or **Top** (straight down, track up), or with the keys 1 to 5. A **Peek** is a drag in a 3D camera: it orbits the camera round the aircraft (Cockpit turns its look instead) and eases back to the camera's framing on release. Terrain is on only in the 3D cameras. A 3D camera picked while the flight is still in its origin's Diorama takes over once it is out of it; arriving, the camera eases into the Journey's own shot and the usual **Handover** follows, into the destination's Approach camera (else Orbit). If WebGL fails or the frame rate stays under about 20 a second, the view **falls back** to Map with a short notice. Below xl a 3D camera frames the aircraft in the **free band**: the strip of map between the Journey card's bottom and the controls at the foot of the screen (`freeBand`, `src/lib/globe/journey-frame.ts`); on a short phone the card folds to its head, switch and progress so the band holds the whole aircraft. While a 3D camera is on, the map also shades the terrain's **relief** (a hillshade) and draws a sky with a horizon, the far ground fading into the horizon's **haze** (drawn by the En-route layer, `hazeShells`); all go on Map. The 3D cameras look past level (the map's tilt limit is 110 while one is on, 78 on Map), with the map's centre moved out along the line of sight (`sightPlacement`), and the address carries the Journey's flat shot meanwhile, so a reload opens on the flat map where the flight is. Code in `src/lib/globe/en-route.ts`, `en-route-journey.ts` (the pure rules), `en-route-controller.ts` (the view's state on the map) and `en-route-layer.ts`.
- **Flight elsewhere**: a flight the search finds on the feed when nothing on screen matches, by callsign, flight number or **tail number** (its registration: N3596W, G-ZBKJ). Picking it opens the built airport it is near, or flies the world map to it and follows it there.
- **Feed**: a public ADS-B source (adsb.lol, with adsb.fi as fallback) read on request. **Feed health** is its online/offline status.
- **Tracker**: turns feed snapshots into tracked aircraft with a state (parked, pushing back, taxiing, holding, climbing, on approach), drawn 12 s behind live (`PLAYBACK_DELAY`) and moved smoothly between fixes. Code in `src/lib/tracker.ts`.
- **Shared tracker**: the page's one geographic Tracker, keyed by hex, kept across airport switches (`SharedSky`, `src/lib/shared-sky.ts`; docs/adr/0001-one-shared-tracker.md). Every poll feeds it as a feed of its own (the airport feed `airport:<code>`, the World map's region reads `region`, the Journey's hex poll `hex:<hex>`), and every view reads it through a feed of its own (the Diorama its airport's aircraft in the airport's metres, the World map every aircraft, the Journey its one flight), all at one moment per frame (`SharedSky.time()`), so an aircraft is at the same place in every view. A frozen recording (`?fixture=1`, `?fixture=sequence`, the map's recorded regions) and the Replay keep trackers of their own.
- **Movements**: departures and arrivals observed by the feed since the page opened; the basis of the board.
- **Replay**: rewinding what the page has seen since it opened (10x or 30x) or a time-lapse of light trails. Nothing is stored server side. The **Seam** is the moment a replay catches up with live: each aircraft eases from its last replay position to its live one under the Tracker's per-frame step bound, instead of jumping (`Seam`, `src/lib/replay.ts`; docs/adr/0001-one-shared-tracker.md). LIVE, a seek and a replay's start are not eased.
- **Fixture**: recorded data used instead of live feeds (`?fixture=1`, `?fixture=sequence`); keeps smoke tests deterministic.
- **Procedure**: an FAA-published approach or departure from CIFP, drawn for the runway in use. **CIFP** is the FAA Coded Instrument Flight Procedures file.
- **Predicted path**: the expected taxi route along an airport's taxiway graph.
- **Squawk**: transponder code; 7700, 7600 and 7500 trigger alerts.
- **METAR**: an airport's weather report, from aviationweather.gov.
- **Ground stop / delay program**: FAA NAS status items shown for an airport.
- **Theme**: light (toy diorama), dark (night ops) or satellite (daylight over aerial imagery).
